Earn Your Place

Rank Progression

Everyone starts at the bottom and works their way up. Put the hours in, pass your assessments and climb the ranks through applications.

STAGE 01

Recruit / Probationer

Complete your induction and basic training. Learn the ropes on supervised shifts with a mentor.

STAGE 02

Response Officer

Cleared for solo patrol. Handle your own calls, build experience and pick your specialism.

STAGE 03

Specialist / Sub-Division

Apply into a specialist sub-division — firearms, traffic, HEMS, HART and more — with extra training.

STAGE 04

Supervisor

Lead teams on the ground, sign off training and take responsibility for your shift.

STAGE 05

Command

Senior leadership — shape the division, run operations and represent the service in staff meetings.

Play Fair

Server Rules

A few of the core rules that keep our roleplay high-quality and fair for everyone. Full rulebook lives in the Discord.

01

Quality RP First

Stay in character, value your life, and treat every scene as if it were real. No fail-RP.

02

Respect Everyone

No toxicity, discrimination or harassment — on the server or in the community. Zero tolerance.

03

No Metagaming

Don't use out-of-character info in character. Stream sniping and mixing OOC/IC is not allowed.

04

New Life Rule

If you're downed and respawn, you forget the events leading to your death. Move on cleanly.

05

Realistic Driving

Drive to the conditions. No unrealistic ramming, GTA-driving or powergaming pursuits.

06

Follow The Chain

On duty, respect ranks and command structure. Off duty, use the right channels for issues.
